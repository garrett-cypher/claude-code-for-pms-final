# Handler interviews: what bears on the 4.2 problem

Source: Customer interviews database (rook-wiki), four console-redesign interviews by the product designer, 2–5 Sep 2026. Interviewees: Aunt Dot (Vesper), Mr. Ambrose (Captain Vantage), Halloran (Sgt. Bulwark), Kip (Meteor Mite, The Gale).

| # | Theme | Raised by | Count |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | The callout is gone before the responder can answer | Aunt Dot, Ambrose, Halloran | 3 of 4 |
| 2 | Alerts are hard for the handler to notice or tell apart | Aunt Dot, Ambrose, Kip | 3 of 4 |
| 3 | Very quiet stretches for some responders, others swamped | Aunt Dot, Kip | 2 of 4 |

## 1. Gone before they could answer (3 of 4)
- Aunt Dot: "It didn't used to feel like a fair race, phone to stairs, and now it does, and he loses it more than he wins it."
- Ambrose: "The young master was halfway into the suit — coat on, one boot on — when it went to somebody else."
- Halloran: "Bulwark mentioned a callout went to someone else before he'd even got his boots on, a few weeks back." Secondhand; dated only "sometime in August".
- Ambrose also said a slower response used to land the job more often than not, and lately it doesn't. His observation, not verified.
- Nobody gave a duration.

## 2. Alerts hard to notice or tell apart (3 of 4)
- Aunt Dot: "I'd just like to know" (she wants a notice herself, not only her responder's phone buzzing through a ceiling).
- Ambrose: "I would want whatever tells me a callout is live to be harder to miss, not easier."
- Kip: "Mite and Gale both just go 'bing' and I have to actually look to know which one."
- A console fix, separate from how fast the ping expires.

## 3. Quiet stretches next to overloaded ones (2 of 4)
- Kip: "Mite thinks Mite's been forgotten. Gale's exhausted."
- Aunt Dot: "There have been stretches, more than one, where the phone just sits there and sits there."
- Kip said it was "all the way to one side or all the way to the other, no middle".
- Neither could say why; neither gave dates.
- Aunt Dot only mentioned the quiet weeks when asked, and only then linked them to the fast-vanishing ones: "I hadn't really put those two things next to each other before."

## Also relevant
- Aunt Dot tells her responder to keep his phone in his pocket all evening so he can beat the vanishing callouts. A handler working around the problem.
- Kip, on the quiet-versus-swamped gap: "Maybe that's not a console thing, maybe that's just... whatever's underneath it."

## What the interviews can't tell us
- Nobody mentioned the 60s ping wait, the ranking change or acceptance. Themes 1 and 3 line up with the working hypotheses, but the interviews don't separate ranking from ping wait.
- Four handlers who agreed to talk is a small sample. Ravi's per-responder weekly numbers are still what can settle it (see `draft-request-to-ravi.md`, items 2 and 6).

## Left out as not applicable
Dark mode (Kip); text and status badge size (Aunt Dot, Ambrose); capability tag legend (Ambrose); filter persistence (Ambrose); Halloran's Supply complaints and his comment on maintenance scheduling.

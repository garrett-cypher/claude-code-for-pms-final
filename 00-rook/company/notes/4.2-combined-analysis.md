## Working hypothesis (untested)

The 90s to 60s ping wait turned 60-90s answers into misses for slower answerers. Misses cost -0.12 each in routing priority with no recovery (2019 TODO in `history.py`), so those responders were pinged less. That gives "gone before I could answer" and, later, "never goes off".

What it does not rest on: no response-time data, no ranking scores, no coordinates, no delivery logs. Rivals still open: push delivery failures, availability or time-zone errors, the proximity weight (0.45 to 0.60) pushing distant responders down.
